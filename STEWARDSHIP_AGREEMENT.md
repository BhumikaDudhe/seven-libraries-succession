## 2. Triggering Conditions
Succession shall be executed by the Council Governance Authority upon the occurrence of any one of the following explicit conditions:

1. **Unresponsiveness (Inactivity Trigger):**
   The primary steward (Ngawang Dorje) fails to acknowledge official catalogue submissions or sign periodic heartbeat beacons for a continuous period exceeding **30 calendar days**.
2. **Voluntary Relinquishment:**
   The primary steward formally submits a signed notice of retirement or resignation to the Council.
3. **Physical Incapacity:**
   The primary steward is medically or physically unable to maintain the catalogue.
4. **Council Supermajority Vote:**
   A vote of at least five of the seven member institutions citing operational negligence or storage lapse.
